## Short in-app acceptance summary

Civora is an invite-only pilot workspace for civil design support and engineer-review packages. It is not construction-ready and does not provide professional engineering services. All outputs are draft, candidate, or review-required materials. Civora does not stamp, seal, sign, certify, approve construction, submit construction documents, or act as engineer of record. You are responsible for verifying sources, standards, assumptions, calculations, geometry, and exports, and for obtaining licensed professional review outside Civora before any reliance.

## Not-for-construction responsibility banner

Not for construction. Civora outputs are draft, review-required materials for engineer review only. Civora does not provide professional engineering services, does not act as engineer of record, and does not stamp, seal, sign, certify, approve, submit, or release construction documents.
