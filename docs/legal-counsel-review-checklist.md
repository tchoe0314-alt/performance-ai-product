# Legal Counsel Review Checklist

Draft status: counsel-review draft. This is not legal advice. Do not publish, send, or rely on this checklist as final legal terms until counsel and the business owner approve it in writing.

This checklist does not enable charging, public beta access, self-serve signup, production launch, or changes to engineering behavior. Civora remains a review-only pilot workspace and does not stamp, seal, sign, certify, approve construction, submit construction documents, or act as engineer of record.

## Review Scope

| Area | Counsel review question | Current draft posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot access | Does invite-only access language match intended onboarding and account approval? | Draft terms limit access to approved pilot users. |
| Review-only boundary | Is the professional responsibility boundary adequate for target jurisdictions? | Draft terms state all outputs are draft, candidate, support, or engineer-review package materials. |
| No professional services | Is no-engineering-services language sufficient? | Draft terms state Civora is software and does not provide professional engineering services. |
| No construction reliance | Is prohibited reliance language enforceable and complete? | Draft terms block use for construction, permitting, bidding, procurement, installation, field work, owner approval, contractor direction, and regulatory submission unless externally reviewed and approved by the responsible licensed professional. |
| Source verification | Does user responsibility for survey/control, standards, map/GIS, CAD, PDF, and imported data cover expected pilot use? | Draft terms require verification and source-rights confirmation. |
| Map/GIS rights | Are source-rights and third-party data restrictions sufficient for maps, GIS exports, imagery, and metadata? | Draft terms say users must have rights to upload, connect, process, display, analyze, and use those sources. |
| AI limitations | Are AI-output disclaimers clear without overstating capability? | Draft terms require review and verification of AI-generated outputs. |
| Customer content | Is the service-operation license appropriate for pilot support and debugging? | Draft terms include a limited service-operation license, subject to final privacy/data-use terms. |
| Confidential data | What confidential, regulated, or client-restricted data may be accepted? | Draft operational docs block confidential input unless owner-approved. |
| Privacy and data processing | Is Civora a processor, service provider, controller, or another role? | Open counsel/business decision. |
| Third-party providers | Are provider disclosures, subprocessors, and flow-down terms adequate? | Draft provider schedule added for review. |
| Security | Are security statements aligned with implemented pilot controls? | Open counsel/business/security decision. |
| Retention and deletion | Are retention periods, deletion SLA, backup exceptions, and legal holds finalized? | Open counsel/business decision. |
| Support and SLA | Should pilot support be no-SLA or limited-SLA? | Draft operational docs require owner choice before charging. |
| Fees and billing | What billing terms, tax, refund, cancellation, and nonpayment language are required if charging is later approved? | Charging remains blocked. This packet does not authorize payment collection. |
| Liability and indemnity | Are caps, exclusions, carveouts, claims periods, and indemnities enforceable? | Open counsel decision. |
| Governing law | What venue, dispute, arbitration, and public-sector exceptions are needed? | Open counsel decision. |
| Notices | What legal, privacy, security, and billing notice contacts are required? | Open counsel/business decision. |

## Counsel-Required Blockers

- Final contracting party, pilot account owner structure, and acceptance mechanics.
- Final privacy policy, data processing terms, and data-use permissions.
- Final confidential input policy and customer authorization process.
- Third-party provider/subprocessor disclosures and any required customer notices.
- Map/GIS, imagery, CAD, PDF, standards, and imported-source rights language.
- Security commitments that match implemented controls.
- Retention, deletion, backup, export, support-copy, and legal-hold terms.
- Jurisdiction-specific professional engineering, unauthorized-practice, and engineer-of-record language.
- Warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability, indemnity, claims, governing law, venue, and dispute language.
- Any paid pilot terms, taxes, refund/cancellation language, payment provider terms, and billing activation approvals.
