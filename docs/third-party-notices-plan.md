# Third-Party Notices Plan

Draft status: counsel-review draft. This is not legal advice. Do not publish notices until counsel, privacy, security, and the business owner approve the content and placement.

This plan does not enable charging, production launch, public beta access, or changes to engineering behavior. It is a risk-reduction plan for notices, attribution, and customer-facing disclosures.

## Notice Inventory

| Notice area | Draft placement | Purpose | Owner | Blocker before publication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot terms acknowledgement | Invite/onboarding flow and terms docs | Confirm invite-only, review-only, not-for-construction use | Product/legal | Counsel-approved terms and acceptance record design. |

| Support attachment warning | Issue-report flow and support docs | Prevent unapproved confidential or restricted data in support channels | Support/legal | Approved support channel and confidential-input policy. |

## Publication Checklist

- Notices use counsel-approved language and do not imply Civora can approve, stamp, seal, sign, certify, submit, or release construction documents.
- Notices do not weaken existing review-only, source-trust, safety, or professional responsibility guardrails.
- Notices do not promise security, deletion, uptime, provider behavior, source accuracy, or legal/code compliance beyond approved terms.
- Map/GIS notices include source-rights, attribution, caching/export, and review-only caveats where required.
- Billing/payment notices stay disabled or draft-only until charging is separately approved.
