# Civora pilot offer and demo — internal draft

Prepared October 13, 2026. Not an approved offer, published agreement, investor traction claim or authorization to invite/charge anyone.

## Offer to finalize

Customer: a boutique civil-engineering/land-development firm performing preliminary office/retail feasibility on a single parcel. First partner, jurisdiction and actual project are not yet supplied.

Purpose: reduce preliminary concept iteration and produce editable geometry for continuation in the firm's existing CAD workflow. Time-saving and no-redrawing claims remain targets until measured independently.

Candidate scope: structured site brief/source records; direct drawing and supported chat edits; bounded geometric alternatives; preliminary supported calculation/quantity outputs; revision-bound preliminary PDF/DXF; project saving, history and view-only-default sharing. Clearly disclose calculations and constraints that are recorded-only, unsupported, stale or unverified. Do not promise verified high-quality AI imagery without authorized fidelity checks.

Responsibility: Civora is not the Engineer of Record. It does not approve, stamp, seal, certify or submit construction documents. Only appropriately responsible professionals decide how preliminary results may be relied on.

Before activation, founder must approve: permitted use and confidential input scope; exact operating limits; pilot duration; price/free-vs-paid choice; trial/conversion/cancellation/refund rules; support contact/coverage; incident owner; approved terms/privacy/retention/deletion; account/billing owner and acceptance record. Reuse existing services; do not create a replacement subscription system without identifying the actual integration.

## Demo sequence

Use a clearly labeled synthetic project until a rights-cleared real project is authorized. Demonstrate only the tested candidate; label a local candidate as local, not live.

1. Show the project brief, units, source evidence and missing requirements.
2. Show parcel/building/parking/access objects and an ordinary direct edit.
3. Undo and redo; show the edit history and affected results becoming stale.
4. Make one equivalent supported chat edit without silently overwriting a sourced target.
5. Compare actual geometry alternatives, explain model limits and explicitly Apply one.
6. Confirm supported affected calculations; show unknown pricing as unknown when no source exists.
7. Save/reopen and verify the selected geometry; demonstrate view-only sharing.
8. Export the saved revision with the correct drawing-only/calculated scope, units and disclosures. PDF overview is fit-to-page, not a measurement plot.
9. Show recorded external CAD continuation only after it has actually been verified.

Do not fabricate a calculation result, hide operator intervention, label geometry checks municipal compliance, or count a download as CAD acceptance. Actual demo recording and full-loop acceptance remain open.

## Pilot measurement record

For each authorized study record: firm/user and accepted use; exact software and project revisions; source identity/permission; baseline workflow/time; Civora active time and generation waits separately; interruptions/manual corrections; export cleanup/redrawing; professional findings; support time; repeat usage; willingness to pay and actual payment/renewal where applicable. Separate unpaid trials, contracted paid pilots and collected revenue.

Start with one partner, fix critical findings, then expand toward the master roadmap's 3–5 firms/10 studies targets. A completed synthetic benchmark is not one of those real studies.
