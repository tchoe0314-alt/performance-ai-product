# Civora Controlled Pilot Terms and Conditions Draft

Draft status: legal/business draft for review. This is not legal advice. Civora should not send, publish, or rely on this draft until reviewed and approved by counsel and the business owner.

These Terms are written for a controlled, invite-only pilot. They do not enable public beta access, self-serve signup, paid use, invoicing, payment collection, production security commitments, or broader commercial launch by themselves.

## 1. Introduction and acceptance

These Controlled Pilot Terms and Conditions govern access to and use of Civora during the controlled pilot. Civora is an AI-assisted civil design and review workflow platform that helps users import project data, draw and edit site objects, use GIS, PDF, CAD, and other source materials, generate civil design support outputs, track blockers and source confidence, and create engineer-review packages.

By accessing or using the pilot, each user and the company or organization they represent agrees to these Terms. If a user does not agree, they must not access or use Civora.

Pilot access is limited to users approved by Civora. Civora may require written acceptance, an order form, a pilot invite, or other account approval before enabling access. These Terms do not grant access by themselves.

## 2. Pilot/beta nature of service

Civora is pilot software. Features may be incomplete, experimental, limited, changed, paused, disabled, or removed during the pilot.

Civora may contain errors, missing information, inaccurate outputs, stale outputs, unsupported file behavior, or incomplete workflow coverage. Pilot users should expect active development and should report issues that affect source trust, safety, exports, review status, or professional responsibility.

The pilot is not a public beta. Public access, self-serve onboarding, broader release, or paid commercial launch requires separate owner approval, operational readiness, legal review, privacy review, billing readiness, and any required product gates.

## 3. Review-only engineering boundary

Civora outputs are draft, candidate, support, analysis, coordination, or engineer-review package materials. They are review-required and are not construction-ready.

Civora may help organize project evidence, assumptions, constraints, blockers, source confidence, geometry, calculations, conflicts, quantities, and exports for review. Civora does not make professional engineering decisions and does not create final approved work.

All Civora outputs must be reviewed by the responsible user, qualified reviewer, and, where required, a licensed professional engineer outside Civora before any reliance.

## 4. No professional engineering services

Civora is software. Civora does not provide professional engineering services through the pilot.

Civora does not stamp, seal, sign, certify, approve construction, submit construction documents, or act as engineer of record. Civora is not a licensed professional engineer, design professional, approval authority, permitting agent, surveyor, code official, or construction administrator.

No Civora output, status, label, workflow, export, report, recommendation, warning, or omission transfers professional responsibility to Civora.

## 5. No construction reliance

Users must not use Civora outputs as construction documents or as a basis for construction, bidding, permitting, procurement, installation, field work, regulatory submission, owner approval, or contractor direction unless the output has been independently reviewed and approved outside Civora by the responsible licensed professional and any other required authority.

Review-package exports, drawings, calculations, quantities, maps, tables, summaries, and design support outputs are not released-for-construction materials.

Users are responsible for ensuring that every construction, submission, or reliance decision is made outside Civora through the user's normal professional review and approval process.

## 6. User responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- using Civora only within the approved pilot scope
- ensuring that authorized users understand these Terms
- providing accurate, current, complete, and lawful project inputs
- verifying all sources, assumptions, geometry, calculations, quantities, conflicts, standards, and outputs
- preserving the review-only boundary in their internal and external communications
- stopping use of affected outputs when a blocker, missing-input warning, stale-output warning, low-confidence source, or safety concern is present
- reporting errors, confusing language, source-trust issues, access issues, data exposure concerns, and responsibility-boundary concerns
- maintaining their own records, backups, professional review files, and approval records

Users must not represent Civora outputs as approved, final, construction-ready, stamped, sealed, signed, certified, submitted, or released by Civora.

## 7. Licensed professional review requirement

Where a project, jurisdiction, client, owner, lender, contractor, permit authority, or applicable law requires professional engineering judgment, users must obtain review from the responsible licensed professional outside Civora.

The licensed professional remains responsible for professional judgment, accepted standards, legal requirements, source verification, project decisions, and any approval, stamping, sealing, signing, submission, or construction release performed outside Civora.

Civora may prepare materials for review, but Civora does not decide whether those materials may be relied on.

## 8. Project data, survey/control, standards, and source verification

Users are responsible for providing and verifying project data, including survey/control, datum, benchmarks, boundary information, easements, existing conditions, utility records, geotechnical information, hydrologic information, jurisdictional requirements, owner requirements, design standards, and client-specific criteria.

Civora may show source confidence, blockers, assumptions, missing inputs, or stale-output warnings. Those signals are aids only. They do not replace user or professional review.

Users must verify that any standard, code, ordinance, specification, or jurisdictional requirement used in Civora is current, applicable, accepted for the project, and correctly interpreted. Civora does not guarantee live legal, code, or standard compliance.

## 9. AI-generated outputs and limitations

Civora uses AI-assisted workflows that may generate or suggest geometry, calculations, assumptions, conflicts, summaries, quantities, issue lists, review package content, and other design support outputs.

AI-generated outputs may be incomplete, incorrect, inconsistent, stale, based on unsupported assumptions, or affected by source limitations. Users must review, verify, and correct AI-generated outputs before reliance.

Civora may produce confident-sounding results even when source information is incomplete. Users must treat all AI-generated output as review-required and must not use Civora as a substitute for professional judgment.

## 10. GIS/PDF/CAD/imported data limitations

GIS, imagery, PDF, CAD, LandXML, spreadsheets, uploaded files, and other imported data may contain errors, omissions, outdated information, scale issues, coordinate-system issues, datum mismatches, parsing errors, layer ambiguity, missing metadata, unsupported entities, or conversion artifacts.

Civora may infer or extract candidate site objects from imported data. Candidate objects are not trusted project evidence unless the user or responsible reviewer verifies and accepts the underlying source.

Users must confirm file provenance, coordinate systems, scale, units, layer meaning, revision status, and completeness before using imported data in project review.

## 11. External approval/stamp records as customer-provided metadata only

Civora may allow users to record external approval, stamp, seal, signature, submission, permit, or release information as customer-provided metadata.

Those records are entered or supplied by the customer, user, or responsible professional outside Civora. Civora does not verify, grant, certify, approve, stamp, seal, sign, submit, or release any project work.

Any external approval or professional record remains the responsibility of the customer and the responsible licensed professional.

## 12. Accounts and authorized users

Pilot access is invite-only and limited to approved accounts and authorized users. Users must keep credentials confidential and must not share accounts.

Each customer is responsible for its users' activity, account administration, access requests, and removal requests. Civora may pause or remove access when users leave the pilot, violate these Terms, create operational risk, or exceed pilot limits.

Civora may require each user to accept these Terms individually, even when an account owner has accepted them for an organization.

## 13. Acceptable use

Users must not:

- use Civora for construction release or unreviewed reliance
- misrepresent Civora as a licensed engineer, approval authority, permitting agent, surveyor, or engineer of record
- upload data they do not have the right to use
- upload regulated, highly sensitive, or restricted data unless Civora has agreed in writing that the data type is allowed for the pilot
- attempt to bypass access controls, usage limits, security controls, review-required labels, warnings, blockers, or pilot restrictions
- reverse engineer, overload, scrape, resell, sublicense, or provide unauthorized access to Civora
- use Civora to develop competing benchmark datasets or model training sets without written permission
- use Civora for unlawful, unsafe, infringing, deceptive, or abusive activity
- remove or hide review-only, not-for-construction, source-confidence, or engineer-review-required language from exported materials

Customers retain ownership of project files, uploaded materials, project inputs, user-created geometry, customer-provided standards, notes, comments, and other content they provide to Civora, subject to any rights of their licensors, clients, consultants, owners, or other third parties.

Customers grant Civora a limited license to host, copy, process, transmit, display, analyze, generate outputs from, and otherwise use customer content as needed to provide, operate, support, secure, debug, improve, and administer the pilot, subject to the agreed pilot scope and any approved privacy or data-use terms.

Civora owns the software, workflows, product design, models, system prompts, templates, documentation, service improvements, and generalized know-how developed independently of customer content.

## 15. Confidential project data handling

If confidential project data is allowed, users must mark or handle it according to the customer's internal policies and any written pilot instructions from Civora. Civora will handle confidential project data according to the final approved pilot privacy, confidentiality, retention, deletion, and support procedures. Until those procedures are finalized, confidential data use should remain limited and controlled.
